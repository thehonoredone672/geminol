Company profile

Chemistry-led, factory-tested, since 1990.

Geminol Lubes Corporation was founded and is developed by Mr. R. Thangavelu, whose analytical-chemistry training set the standard the works still runs on: nothing ships until the compounding checks out on the bench.

RT
"Molecular balancing isn't a slogan here — it's the actual bench work behind every commercial lot."

Mr. R. Thangavelu, M.Sc. (Analytical Chemistry), B.Ed. has specialised in the lubricants industry since June 1990. His academic grounding in analytical chemistry carries directly into the works: he oversees every compounding stage himself to keep molecular balancing precise and consistent across production runs.

Years spent across multiple lubricant plants, handling advanced laboratory instrumentation first-hand, shape how quality control runs at Geminol today — practical factory experience checked against rigorous lab method, batch by batch.

Before a batch ships

Four checks, every lot.

01

Raw material check

Base stock and additives are verified against specification before compounding starts — nothing enters the mix unchecked.

02

Compounding & molecular balancing

Mr. Thangavelu oversees the blend ratios directly, working to film stability and shear resistance targets rather than a fixed recipe alone.

03

Laboratory testing

Advanced in-house lab instrumentation checks the compounded batch before it's cleared — the same equipment and method, run consistently.

04

Batch release

Only a batch that clears laboratory testing is packed and dispatched, in the pail, barrel or tanker quantity the order calls for.
